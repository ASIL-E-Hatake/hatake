## 0.0.1

- Initial release.
- `MaterialRenderer`: a Material 3 renderer for `CrudPageDefinition` and
  `SearchPageDefinition`.
- Search filters, sortable data table, pagination, row edit/delete, and a
  create/edit form shown as a dialog with validation feedback.
- Form field types: text, textarea, number, select, checkbox, radio,
  multiSelect, and date (date picker).
- Custom field types via `fieldBuilders`.
